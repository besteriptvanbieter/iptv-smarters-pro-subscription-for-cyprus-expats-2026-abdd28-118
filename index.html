How to Stop Buffering and Throttling

Sometimes your Internet Service Provider (ISP) slows down video streams. This is called throttling. You can stop this easily.

  1. Change your DNS: Use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1). It is faster and more private.
  2. Adjust Buffer Size: In your IPTV player settings, set the buffer or cache to 5,000 ms. This stores more data before playing, so your video does not freeze.
